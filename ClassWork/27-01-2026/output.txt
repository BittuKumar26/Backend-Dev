Pawan Singh (born January 5, 1986) is a renowned Indian playback singer and actor who revolutionized Bhojpuri cinema and music, famously known for the 2008 global hit song "Lollypop Lagelu". Born in Arrah, Bihar, he began his career with the album Odhaniya Wali in 1997, becoming one of Bhojpuri cinema's most bankable stars

Key Career and Historical Highlights:
Early Life & Music: Singh showed interest in music from a young age, launching his singing career with Odhaniya Wali at age 11.
Breakthrough: His 2008 album Lollypop Lagelu achieved immense, worldwide popularity, making him an overnight star.
Film Career: He made his acting debut in the 2007 film Rangli Chunariya Tohre Naam and has starred in major hits like Pratigya, Dacait, Ziddi Aashiq, and Ghatak.
Awards: He was honored with the Best Male Singer award at the International Bhojpuri Film Awards in 2016.
Bollywood Debut: In 2020, he made his Hindi music debut with the song "Kamariya Hila Rahi Hai" featuring Lauren Gottlieb.
Controversies & Personal Life: Known for his high-profile career, he has also faced public scrutiny regarding his relationships, including with actress Akshara Singh, and his divorces.
